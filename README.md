Welcome to A Bit Spacey, my first ever programming and game design project!

Quick note: if you want to play A Bit Spacey, download all of the files into a folder and then open the "game.exe" file.

  This project, being my first project ever, was my senior project in high school and took just over 35 hours to make. It served as my introduction to programming an actual project and the process of designing a game. Said process also involved learning Pygame by looking online in their catalogues of functions and watching YouTube vides online, as it was my first experience with them. 
  Admittedly, it's a small and (for now) slightly buggy game, but my focus is currently on other projects. I may come back to patch up the code at some point, but for now A Bit Spacey showcases the very beginning of my learning process.

  The controls to play are as follows:
[Arrow keys] to move
[Space] to shoot
[\] to do...something...
